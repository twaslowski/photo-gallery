## Reflections

Since Dresden, we've been to two places. Bamberg, for two weeks; and Munich, for a bit less than that.
We intended to stay longer in Munich than we ended up doing, because we had some paperwork to get to back home.
Even as digital nomads, doctor's appointments and bureaucracy will force you back more often than you would think;
at least that has been our experience up to this point.

I do not have a lot of photos from Bamberg. We stayed some 20km outside the city, fairly far out in a tiny
village. Most of our walks were not as scenic as they often are. When we did go to Bamberg, my photography
was not exactly up to par; there is essentially nothing I have from there that is worth posting.

This is also a reason this post is so substantially delayed: It took me a while to come to terms
with the fact that there are places I could not take any good pictures of. In the end, I decided
I want this to be a photography rather than a travel blog (hence the domain), and therefore it'd be
fine to not have pictures of a place.

The situation is only slightly better for Munich. To be completely honest, a lot of energy that I previously put into
photography is currently being diverted to work, so there's not going to be as much. When we go out to see something
truly scenic, I'll capture some cool photos, but my motivation to take the camera out on a day-to-day basis and really
invest the time to find the small things has suffered a bit.

I suppose this is one of the many cases of my inability to focus on a single thing for an extended period of time.
I experience this with music, going to the gym, learning languages, chess, my pet programming projects – virtually every
aspect of my life. It turns out I can get reasonably proficient at almost anything, but then
my motivation for it kind of drops off eventually when I find the next thing. Right now, I'm more focused on my job
and learning Russian, for the practical reason that I'm getting married next March and my current Russian is not
sufficient to have proper conversations with my fiancèes side of the family.

We didn't do anything particularly post-worthy in August except for a trip to New York, which turned out to
be _very_ post-worthy, and after getting through all the editing I'm very excited for the next post!
However, my work on this blog will continue to be infrequent, so let's see when you get those pictures.

Alright, with all of that personal stuff out of the way, let's get into it!

## The last Dresden photos

Before I get to Bavaria: On our last day in Dresden, we went to the most famous part of the sandstone mountains,
and I got a few nice shots I'd be remiss not to include in this post.

We went to the Bastei, the most well-known structure within the Dresden sandstone mountains, on our last day in
Dresden. Unfortunately, the weather was not quite on our side, and it ended up being very cloudy. Therefore, the
images I ended up getting are very moody. They do have character though – and they do look much better if your
browser and screen support HDR ;)
